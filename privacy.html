Last updated: 2 October 2026

About this website

Channel AI is an educational website maintained by Chel (Chel Koby), with articles and projects about AI, machine learning, computer vision, and virtual reality. This notice applies to channelai.netlify.app.

Information from your visit

When your browser requests a page, the hosting and delivery services receive technical information needed to respond, such as your IP address and the requested address. Depending on their settings, they may also process browser information and request logs for operation, security, and diagnostics.

The website is hosted on Netlify. You can read about its handling of personal information in Netlify's privacy statement.

Third-party services and advertising

Pages load some code from external services, including libraries and icons delivered through content delivery networks. Your browser contacts those services when it downloads their files, so they receive request information such as your IP address.

The website includes Google AdSense code. Google and other advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure ads. Google's advertising cookies can enable it and its partners to serve ads based on visits to this website and other websites.

You can manage personalised advertising through Google My Ad Center. For more information, see how Google uses information from sites that use its services and participating advertising providers’ opt-out options. These controls do not necessarily stop all advertising or every form of data processing.

External links

Articles and project pages link to services such as GitHub, Google Colab, Coursera, and LinkedIn. Following a link takes you to a separate service whose privacy notice and terms apply. An ordinary link is different from content embedded directly into this website.

Contacting the site owner

If you email the site owner, your email address, message, and any information you choose to share are processed to receive and respond to your enquiry. Avoid sending passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information.

For privacy enquiries, contact cheloverboard@gmail.com. You can ask about information associated with your correspondence and request access, correction, or deletion where applicable. Enough information may be needed to identify the relevant correspondence and confirm that the request is yours.

Your choices

Your browser settings can control or remove cookies and other stored website data. You can also use the provider controls linked above. Blocking cookies or external resources may affect how some features work.

Changes to this notice

This notice will be revised when the site's features or use of third-party services changes. The update date above identifies the latest revision.
